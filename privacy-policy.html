Privacy Policy — Your Data Stays on Your Device

Last updated: 22 October 2026

The short version

Our tools run entirely in your web browser. Files you open (photos, text, dates) are processed on your own device and are never uploaded, stored or shared by SSToolHub. This site does not offer user accounts.

What we collect

We do not ask for accounts, names or personal information to use any tool, and the tools themselves send us nothing: the photo you compress, the text you count and the dates you enter never leave your device.

Server logs

Like almost every website, this site is delivered by a hosting provider that keeps standard server logs for security and basic statistics. A log entry typically records the page requested, the time of the request, your browser type and version, and the internet (IP) address the request came from. These logs are generated automatically by the hosting platform; we do not use them to identify individual visitors, we do not combine them with any other data (we hold none), and we never sell or share them. Retention is set by the hosting provider's own policies.

Advertising

This site may display advertising (for example Google AdSense). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Advertising partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services on Google's partner sites page, and you can opt out of personalised advertising in your Google account settings (adssettings.google.com).

Cookies

SSToolHub itself sets no tracking cookies. Third-party advertisers, if present, may set their own cookies as described above.

Your choices

You stay in control of advertising cookies: you can opt out of personalised advertising in your Google account settings, block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings, and use your browser's private/incognito mode if you prefer not to keep cookies between visits. Blocking cookies does not affect the tools themselves — every tool on this site works without any cookie at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Questions about privacy: see the Contact page.

Privacy Policy

The short version

Our tools run entirely in your web browser. Files you open (photos, text, dates) are processed on your own device and are never uploaded, stored or shared by SSToolHub.

What we collect

We do not ask for accounts, names or personal information to use any tool. Like most websites, standard server logs (such as page visited and browser type) may be kept by our hosting provider for security and statistics.

Advertising

This site may display advertising (for example Google AdSense). Advertising partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your Google account settings (adssettings.google.com).

Cookies

SSToolHub itself sets no tracking cookies. Third-party advertisers, if present, may set their own cookies as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Questions about privacy: see the Contact page.

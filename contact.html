Contact Us — Suggestions, Bugs and Tool Requests

We read every message. Use the contact route below for:

  • A tool you wish existed — most of our new tools come from requests
  • A wrong result or a bug you spotted
  • Business or advertising questions

Note: because all tools run in your browser, we never receive your files — please do not email photos or documents; describe the issue instead.

Reporting a bug or a wrong result

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include: the name of the tool, what you entered, what result you expected, what result you got instead, and the device and browser you used (for example "Android phone, Chrome"). A screenshot of the result panel helps a lot — but never send personal documents or photos.

Suggesting a new tool

Tell us the job you are trying to finish — "I need to shrink my photo to exactly 50KB for a job form" is the kind of message that becomes a tool here. If a tool can run privately in a browser and helps many people, it goes on our build list.

Business and advertising

For partnership or advertising questions, please write "Business" in the subject line so your message is routed correctly. We reply to genuine enquiries in the order they arrive.

What is SSToolHub?

SSToolHub is a small, focused collection of free online tools for the jobs people actually search for: shrinking a photo to the exact size an application form demands, checking an exact age against a job cut-off, working out a percentage before an admission deadline, pricing a loan before signing it, making a QR code for a shop counter, counting the words in an essay. Every tool does one job, does it immediately, and asks for nothing in return — no account, no email address, no trial period, and no watermark on anything you download.

Who these tools help

Job applicants and students use the image tools most: government and exam portals accept photos only under fixed file sizes (20KB, 50KB, 100KB, 200KB) and in exact shapes like 35×45mm, and a normal phone photo fails both tests until it passes through the right tool. Students check marks percentages and count essay words against assignment limits. Shop owners and small businesses generate QR codes for menus, payment links and WhatsApp ordering, and check loan EMIs before borrowing for stock or equipment. Everyone else arrives with a date to count, a password to strengthen, or a percentage to settle — everyday questions that deserve an instant, trustworthy answer instead of a sign-up wall.

How the tools work — and why your files stay private

Most tool websites upload your file to their server, process it there, and let you download the result. SSToolHub works the other way: every tool is a small program that runs entirely inside your own browser, on your own device. Your photo is compressed by your phone, not by our server; your text is counted locally; your dates never leave the page. That design is why the tools are instant, why they keep working on a slow connection, and why we can honestly promise that your files are never uploaded, stored or shared — there is no point in the process where we could see them, even if we wanted to. You can prove it yourself: open any tool, switch your phone to flight mode, and watch it carry on working.

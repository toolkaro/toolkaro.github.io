Date Difference Calculator — days between dates

Deadlines, visa validity, project timelines — counted exactly.

Common uses

  • How many days are left on a visa, warranty or subscription
  • Days between an application date and a deadline
  • Project timelines and payment schedules
  • Exact age-style spans (for age itself, use our Age Calculator)

How it counts

The result shows the full span in years, months and days (the way people naturally read it) and also the exact total in days and weeks — the way forms and contracts count it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does it include both the start and end day?

The count is the exact number of full days between the two dates — the standard method used by forms and contracts.

Q: What if I enter the dates in the wrong order?

No problem — the calculator automatically treats the earlier date as the start.

Q: Can I count days until a future date?

Yes. Put today as the start date and the future date as the end date.

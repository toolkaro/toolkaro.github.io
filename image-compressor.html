Image Compressor — smaller files, same clear picture

Shrink photos for websites, WhatsApp, email and uploads — without losing visible quality.

When do you need to compress an image?

Big photos are slow to upload, rejected by forms and websites, and eat phone storage. Compressing reduces the file size — often by 70–90% — while the picture still looks the same on screen.

Quality vs size

At 80% quality most photos shrink a lot with no visible change. Go lower (50–60%) when you need the smallest possible file, for example for a slow connection or a strict upload limit. If your form asks for an exact KB size, use our Compress Image to KB tool instead.

Private by design

Your image is processed on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, stored or shared.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much smaller will my image get?

Most phone photos shrink 70–90% at 80% quality with no visible difference.

Q: Does compression change the dimensions?

No — this tool keeps the same width and height and only reduces the file size. For an exact KB target, use the Compress Image to KB tool.

Q: Which formats are supported?

JPG and PNG images. The compressed result is always a JPG, which every website and form accepts.

Q: Is it really private?

Yes. The image never leaves your device; the compression runs in your browser.

Passport Size Photo Maker (35 × 45 mm)

Make a form-ready passport photo from any picture — correct size, correct proportions.

The official passport photo size

Most countries and form portals use the 35mm × 45mm size (about 1.38 × 1.77 inches). Your face should fill most of the frame, looking straight at the camera, with a plain light background in the original photo.

How to make your passport photo

  1. Upload a clear front-facing photo.
  2. Use Zoom so your face fills the frame, then use the move sliders to centre your head and shoulders.
  3. Download — the photo is saved at print quality (413 × 531 px at 300 DPI).

Need a specific file size too?

Many online forms also demand the photo be under 50KB or 200KB. After downloading, run it through our Compress Image to KB tool to hit the exact size.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What size is a passport photo?

35mm × 45mm in most countries. This tool crops to exactly that ratio and exports at 300 DPI print quality (413 × 531 pixels).

Q: Can this tool change my background to white?

It places your photo on a white canvas, but it cannot remove a busy background from the photo itself. For official use, take your original photo against a plain light wall.

Q: My form also asks for under 50KB. What do I do?

Download the passport photo, then use our Compress Image to KB tool to set it to exactly 50KB or any size your form requires.

Q: Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser on your own device.

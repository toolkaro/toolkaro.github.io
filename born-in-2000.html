Born in 2000 — How Old Are You in 2026?

Born in 2000? You turn 26 in 2026. Your age in days, weeks and months, Generation Z and Year of the Dragon facts, famous 2000 birthdays — and the exact-age calculator.

How old is someone born in 2000?

If you were born in 2000, you turn 26 in 2026 — you are 25 until your 2026 birthday arrives, and 26 from that day onwards. People born in 2000 belong to Generation Z, and in the Chinese zodiac, 2000 is the Year of the Dragon.

Generation Z has never known a world without the internet, smartphones and social media. They are true digital natives: quick with new technology, visual in how they communicate, and connected to the whole world from their pocket.

The world in 2000

The year you were born was anything but quiet. Three moments from 2000 that the world still talks about:

  • The world celebrates the new millennium as the feared Y2K bug fizzles out.
  • Sony launches the PlayStation 2, later the best-selling console ever.
  • The first crew arrives to live aboard the International Space Station.

Famous people born in 2000

You share your birth year with some of the most recognised names on the planet: Addison Rae, Willow Smith — all born in 2000, all known around the world.

2000 on screen and on the radio

The film that defined 2000 was Gladiator, and the song the world could not stop playing was Bye Bye Bye — NSYNC. If you were born in 2000, both were already classics by the time you were old enough to notice them.

A sporting moment from 2000

The Sydney Olympics of 2000 are fondly remembered — above all for Cathy Freeman, an Indigenous Australian athlete who lit the Olympic flame and then won the 400 metres before a roaring home crowd. In football, France added the European Championship to their World Cup crown, becoming the first reigning world champions also to hold the European title.

Technology in 2000

Sony's PlayStation 2 launched in 2000 and became the best-selling games console in history — doubling, in many homes, as the family's first DVD player. The first true camera phone, Sharp's J-SH04, went on sale in Japan, and USB flash drives appeared on the market, beginning the floppy disk's long goodbye from everyday computing.

Everyday life in 2000

The dot-com share bubble peaked and burst in 2000, but daily life kept digitising regardless: a cinema ticket in the US averaged about $5.40, households were emailing photographs to each other as chunky attachments, and the text message quietly overtook the phone call for a generation of teenagers coordinating their social lives. The world partied into a new century — London opened its Millennium Dome, and the first sunrise of 2000 was broadcast live from Pacific islands. In July, a Concorde crashed near Paris, the type's only fatal accident, beginning its path to retirement.

Picture the world a 2000 baby arrived into. The world celebrates the new millennium as the feared Y2K bug fizzles out. At the cinema, everyone was talking about Gladiator; on the radio, Bye Bye Bye — NSYNC was everywhere. And somewhere that same year, Addison Rae was born — a future household name who would grow up right alongside you.

The world since 2000

Being born in 2000 means you have already lived through 7 leap years, 7 FIFA World Cups — the first one after your 10th birthday kicked off in 2010, and 7 Summer Olympic Games. The world has gained billions of people, shrunk onto screens in every pocket, and changed almost everything — except the question that brought you here: how old am I, exactly?

Your life on a timeline

A 2000 birth, year by year: you started school at about five, in 2005. You turned 10 in 2010 — the last single-digit year gone. You turned 18 in 2018, the birthday that makes you an adult in most of the world, and turned 21 in 2021 soon after. Milestone birthdays since: you will turn 30 in 2030, you will turn 40 in 2040, and you will turn 50 in 2050. Looking ahead, you will turn 60 in 2060 and — the one every family hopes to celebrate — you will turn 100 in 2100.

The world then and now

When you arrived in 2000, roughly 6.1 billion people shared the planet. Today the world population is about 8.3 billion — around 2.2 billion more people have been added during your lifetime alone. In 2000 there was no phone in most pockets and no web page to read this on; now the average person born in 2000 has lived to see the internet arrive, phones become cameras, maps and banks, and the first widely used AI tools — all within one lifetime.

Chinese zodiac: Year of the Dragon

2000 is the Year of the Dragon in the 12-year Chinese zodiac cycle. One caution: the Chinese year begins at Chinese New Year, in late January or February, so if your birthday falls in January or early February 2000, your sign may actually be the previous year's animal.

Age Calculator — exact age from your date of birth

Years, months and days — plus total days lived and your next birthday countdown.

How to use this age calculator

Enter your date of birth, then press Calculate My Age. You instantly get your exact age in years, months and days, the total number of days you have lived, how many weeks and hours that makes, and how many days are left until your next birthday.

How age is calculated — the method

Age is counted the way official forms count it: completed years first, then completed months, then the remaining days. The calculator takes your birth date and counts forward. First it counts full years up to your last birthday. Then it counts full calendar months from that birthday. Whatever is left over is counted in days. Because months have different lengths — 28, 29, 30 or 31 days — the "remaining days" step always borrows the true length of the month before the target date, and leap years (including the leap day of 29 February) are handled automatically.

Total days lived are counted separately and exactly: every calendar day between your birth date and the target date, with no rounding. That is why two people who are both "30 years and 5 months" old can have different day totals — it depends on which months and leap years fall inside their span.

A worked example

Suppose you were born on 15 August 1998 and today is 10 October 2026. Your last birthday was 15 August 2026, so you have completed 28 full years. From 15 August to 15 September is one full month, and from 15 September to 10 October is 25 days. Your exact age is therefore 28 years, 1 month and 25 days — and the calculator will also show that you have lived a little over 10,280 days in total.

Checking age for a job, school or exam?

Most government jobs, schools, universities and exams set an age limit on a specific date — for example "age must be under 25 on 31 December" or "the candidate must be 18 on the first day of the course". Change the second date in this calculator to that cut-off date and it tells you exactly what your age will be on that day, so you can check your eligibility in seconds instead of guessing. This matters more than people expect: being a few days over a cut-off is a common — and completely avoidable — reason for rejected applications.

Common mistakes when working out age by hand

  • Just subtracting the years. 2026 − 1998 = 28, but if your birthday has not happened yet this year, you are still 27. The months and days decide.
  • Assuming every month has 30 days. Hand counts often go wrong around February and the 31-day months.
  • Forgetting leap years. Over 28 years there are about 7 leap days; ignoring them makes a day-count wrong by a week.
  • Using the wrong cut-off. Eligibility usually depends on your age on a fixed date in the rules, not your age today.

When an exact age matters

Exact age checks appear in job applications, school and university admissions, competitive exams, sports categories, visa and ID applications, pension and insurance start dates, and driving-licence rules. In all of them, "about" is not good enough — one day can change the answer, which is why an exact day-level calculator is the safe way to check.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this age calculator free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up. Use it as many times as you like.

Q: Can I check my age on a specific date?

Yes. Change the second date to any cut-off date — for example a job application deadline or the first day of a course — and the calculator shows your exact age on that day.

Q: Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to any server and nothing is saved when you close the page.

Q: Does it handle leap years?

Yes. Leap years, the 29 February leap day and the different lengths of months are all handled automatically, both in the years/months/days result and in the total-days count.

Q: Why does my age in days not match years × 365?

Because some years have 366 days. A span of 28 years normally contains about 7 leap days, so an exact day count is always a few days more than years multiplied by 365.

Q: How old am I in months?

Multiply your completed years by 12 and add the completed months. The calculator shows years, months and days together; for example 28 years and 1 month is 337 months.

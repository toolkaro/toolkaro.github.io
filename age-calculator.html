Age Calculator — exact age from your date of birth

Years, months and days — plus total days lived and your next birthday countdown.

How to use this age calculator

Enter your date of birth, then press Calculate My Age. You instantly get your exact age in years, months and days, the total number of days you have lived, and how many days are left until your next birthday.

Checking age for a job, school or exam?

Most government jobs, schools and exams set an age limit on a specific date — for example "age must be under 25 on 31 December". Change the second date to that cut-off date and this calculator tells you exactly what your age will be on that day, so you can check your eligibility in seconds.

How age is calculated

Age is counted in completed years first, then completed months, then the remaining days — the same method used on official forms. Leap years are handled automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this age calculator free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up. Use it as many times as you like.

Q: Can I check my age on a specific date?

Yes. Change the second date to any cut-off date — for example a job application deadline — and the calculator shows your exact age on that day.

Q: Is my date of birth stored anywhere?

No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to any server.

Q: Does it handle leap years?

Yes, leap years and different month lengths are handled automatically.

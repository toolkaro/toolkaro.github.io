Marks Percentage Calculator — for students

Enter each subject's marks and get your total, percentage and grade in one click.

How percentage is calculated

Percentage = (Total marks obtained ÷ Total maximum marks) × 100. This calculator adds all your subjects together and applies exactly that formula.

Grading scale used

90% and above: A+ · 80–89%: A · 70–79%: B · 60–69%: C · 50–59%: D · 40–49%: E · Below 40%: F. Your board or university may use a slightly different scale, but the percentage is always calculated the same way.

Useful for

Checking results, filling admission forms that ask for your percentage, and converting marks when applying for jobs or scholarships.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I calculate my percentage from marks?

Add all the marks you obtained, divide by the total maximum marks of all subjects, and multiply by 100. This calculator does it for you automatically.

Q: Can I use it for one subject only?

Yes — just fill in one row.

Q: What grade is 75%?

On the common scale used here, 70–79% is a B grade. Your own board may grade differently, but your percentage stays the same.

Q: Is this calculator free?

Yes, completely free and unlimited.

QR Code Generator — free, no watermark

For shop counters, menus, business cards, posters and payment pages.

Where QR codes are used

Shops put them on counters for menus and payment links, businesses print them on cards and flyers so customers reach a website or WhatsApp in one scan, and schools and offices use them for forms and attendance links.

Tips for a QR code that always scans

  • Print it at least 2.5cm wide.
  • Keep strong contrast — dark code on a light background.
  • Test it with your own phone before printing many copies.
  • For WhatsApp, paste your full chat link (https://wa.me/your-number).

Free forever

The QR codes made here never expire and have no watermark, because a QR code is simply your text drawn as a pattern — there is no server and no tracking link in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do QR codes from here expire?

No. The code contains your text directly, so it works forever and has no watermark or tracking.

Q: How do I make a QR code for WhatsApp?

Type your WhatsApp link — https://wa.me/ followed by your number with country code (for example https://wa.me/923001234567) — and generate.

Q: What size should I choose?

Use Medium for screens and sharing, and Large (1024px) for printing posters, banners and shop boards.

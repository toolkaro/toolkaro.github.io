The three percentage formulas

Percentage of a number: (percent ÷ 100) × number. Example: 20% of 500 = 100.

One number as a percent of another: (first ÷ second) × 100. Example: 45 is 22.5% of 200.

Percentage change: ((new − old) ÷ old) × 100. A positive result is an increase, negative is a decrease. Example: from 100 to 125 is a 25% increase.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I find a percentage of a number?

Divide the percent by 100 and multiply by the number. For example, 20% of 500 = 0.20 × 500 = 100. Mode 1 above does this for you.

Q: How is percentage increase calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100.

Q: Can this calculate discounts?

Yes — use mode 1: a 30% discount on 2,000 means you save 600, so you pay 1,400.

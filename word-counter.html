Word Counter — live word & character count

For essays, articles, assignments and social media posts with limits.

What this counter measures

Words (separated by spaces), characters with and without spaces — important for forms and platforms with character limits — plus sentences, paragraphs, and how long the text takes to read (at an average 200 words per minute) and to speak aloud (about 130 words per minute — handy for speeches and video scripts).

Common limits people check here

  • School & university essays (500 / 1,000 / 2,000 words)
  • Personal statements (~4,000 characters)
  • Video scripts (about 130 words = 1 minute of speech)
  • Articles and blog posts for SEO (1,000+ words)

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How are words counted?

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces — the same method used by Microsoft Word and Google Docs.

Q: Why do I need characters without spaces?

Some forms and platforms (for example university personal statements and SMS) count characters, sometimes excluding spaces. Both counts are shown.

Q: How is speaking time calculated?

Average speech is about 130 words per minute, so a 650-word script is roughly a 5-minute talk or video.

Q: Is my text saved or sent anywhere?

No. Counting happens in your browser; your text never leaves your device.

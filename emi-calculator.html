EMI Calculator — monthly loan payment

For bike, car, personal and business loans — know the real cost before you sign.

What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay every month until a loan is finished. Each payment covers part interest and part of the borrowed amount (principal).

The EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the number of months.

Before taking any loan

  • Compare the total interest, not just the monthly payment — a longer duration means a smaller EMI but much more interest overall.
  • Ask about processing fees and early-repayment penalties.
  • Keep total monthly installments comfortably below your monthly income.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What does EMI include?

Each monthly payment includes interest for that month plus a part of the principal. Early payments are mostly interest; later payments are mostly principal.

Q: Does a longer loan duration save money?

It lowers the monthly payment but increases the total interest a lot. Compare the "Total interest" line before deciding.

Q: Can I use this for a car or bike loan?

Yes — any fixed-rate loan: personal, car, bike, home or business.

Q: Is the result exact?

It matches the standard bank formula. Your bank may add fees or insurance, which would increase the real cost slightly.

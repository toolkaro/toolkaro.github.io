# SSToolHub — Free Online Tools

**Free online tools that run 100% in your browser. No sign-up, no uploads, no watermarks.**

🌐 **Live site: https://toolkaro.github.io**

SSToolHub is a collection of free, privacy-first online tools. Every tool runs entirely on your own device — your photos, files and text are never uploaded to a server. The site is plain static HTML with zero backend: open a tool, get your answer, done.

## Free Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Age Calculator](https://toolkaro.github.io/age-calculator.html) | Exact age in years, months and days — plus total days lived and your next birthday |
| [Compress Image to KB](https://toolkaro.github.io/compress-image-to-kb.html) | Compress an image to an exact size — 20KB, 50KB, 100KB, 200KB or custom — for job forms, exam forms and online applications |
| [Image Compressor](https://toolkaro.github.io/image-compressor.html) | Reduce JPG and PNG file size by up to 90% while keeping the picture clear |
| [Passport Size Photo Maker](https://toolkaro.github.io/passport-size-photo-maker.html) | Crop any photo to the official 35×45mm passport size at print quality |
| [Marks Percentage Calculator](https://toolkaro.github.io/marks-percentage-calculator.html) | Total marks, percentage and grade for students, subject by subject |
| [Percentage Calculator](https://toolkaro.github.io/percentage-calculator.html) | Percentage of a number, one number as a percent of another, and percentage increase/decrease |
| [Password Generator](https://toolkaro.github.io/password-generator.html) | Strong random passwords generated locally on your device — never sent anywhere |
| [QR Code Generator](https://toolkaro.github.io/qr-code-generator.html) | Free QR codes for links, text and WhatsApp — no watermark, never expire |
| [Word Counter](https://toolkaro.github.io/word-counter.html) | Words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, reading time and speaking time — live as you type |
| [EMI Calculator](https://toolkaro.github.io/emi-calculator.html) | Monthly loan payment (EMI), total interest and total cost for any loan |
| [Date Difference Calculator](https://toolkaro.github.io/date-difference-calculator.html) | Exact days, weeks and months between any two dates |

## About SSToolHub

SSToolHub exists for the everyday jobs people actually search for: a form that demands a photo under 50KB, a student who needs a percentage before an admission deadline, a shop that needs a QR code today. Every tool is free forever, works instantly on any phone or computer, and respects your privacy by design — because all processing happens in your browser, we never see, store or share your files.

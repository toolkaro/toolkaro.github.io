# SSToolHub — Free Online Tools

**Live site: https://toolkaro.github.io/**

Free online tools that run 100% in your browser — no sign-up, no uploads, your files never leave your device.

## Tools
- **Age Calculator** — exact age in years, months and days, plus age on any specific date (job & school age limits)
- **Compress Image to KB** — shrink a photo to an exact size (20KB / 50KB / 100KB / 200KB) for online forms
- **Image Compressor** — make any JPG/PNG file smaller without visible quality loss
- **Passport Size Photo Maker** — crop any photo to the official 35×45mm size
- **Marks Percentage Calculator** — subject-wise marks to percentage and grade
- **Percentage Calculator** — percentage of a number, percent share, percentage increase/decrease
- **Password Generator** — strong random passwords, generated on your device
- **QR Code Generator** — free QR codes for links, text and WhatsApp numbers, no watermark
- **Word Counter** — words, characters, sentences, reading and speaking time
- **EMI Calculator** — monthly loan installments, total interest and total cost
- **Date Difference Calculator** — exact days between any two dates

Plus a "Born in …" age guide for every birth year from 1975 to 2015.

## Why SSToolHub?
- Free forever, no accounts, no watermarks
- Private by design — every tool processes data locally in the browser
- Fast, lightweight pages that work on any phone

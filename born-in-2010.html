Born in 2010 — How Old Are You in 2026?

Born in 2010? You turn 16 in 2026. Your age in days, weeks and months, Generation Z and Year of the Tiger facts, famous 2010 birthdays — and the exact-age calculator.

How old is someone born in 2010?

If you were born in 2010, you turn 16 in 2026 — you are 15 until your 2026 birthday arrives, and 16 from that day onwards. People born in 2010 belong to Generation Z, and in the Chinese zodiac, 2010 is the Year of the Tiger.

Generation Z has never known a world without the internet, smartphones and social media. They are true digital natives: quick with new technology, visual in how they communicate, and connected to the whole world from their pocket.

The world in 2010

The year you were born was anything but quiet. Three moments from 2010 that the world still talks about:

  • Apple launches the iPad tablet.
  • Instagram launches and photo sharing explodes.
  • South Africa hosts the first FIFA World Cup held in Africa.

Famous people born in 2010

You share your birth year with some of the most recognised names on the planet: Diana (Kids Diana Show), Nidal Wonder — all born in 2010, all known around the world.

2010 on screen and on the radio

The film that defined 2010 was Toy Story 3, and the song the world could not stop playing was Love the Way You Lie — Eminem ft. Rihanna. If you were born in 2010, both were already classics by the time you were old enough to notice them.

Ask people who remember 2010 and the same things come back. Apple launches the iPad tablet. Toy Story 3 was the film of the moment, Love the Way You Lie — Eminem ft. Rihanna was the song of the moment, and for Diana (Kids Diana Show) — born that year, just like you — life was just beginning.

The world since 2010

Being born in 2010 means you have already lived through 4 leap years, 5 FIFA World Cups — the first one after your 10th birthday kicked off in 2022, and 4 Summer Olympic Games. The world has gained billions of people, shrunk onto screens in every pocket, and changed almost everything — except the question that brought you here: how old am I, exactly?

Chinese zodiac: Year of the Tiger

2010 is the Year of the Tiger in the 12-year Chinese zodiac cycle. One caution: the Chinese year begins at Chinese New Year, in late January or February, so if your birthday falls in January or early February 2010, your sign may actually be the previous year's animal.
